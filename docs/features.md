# Features

FreeQueues provides a robust set of features tailored for both business administrators and customers.

## For Businesses

### 1. Multi-Branch Management
-   Manage multiple business locations from a single dashboard.
-   Configure specific hours and services for each branch.

### 2. Service Point Configuration
-   Create and manage counters (gichets/counters) within branches.
-   Assign specific staff members to counters.
-   Set counter-specific services (e.g., "Counter 1 - Payments only").

### 3. Real-Time Dashboard
-   Live view of all current queues.
-   View customer details and wait times.
-   "Call next customer" functionality with instant WebSocket notification.

### 4. Operational Analytics
-   Detailed statistics on average wait times, peak hours, and staff performance.
-   Exportable reports for business intelligence.

## For Customers

### 1. Remote Queue Joining
-   Browse businesses and branches on the map or list view.
-   Join a queue from anywhere via the Web or Mobile app.
-   Receive an instant digital ticket with your position.

### 2. Real-Time Status Tracking
-   Monitor your position in the queue live.
-   Receive estimated wait time updates.
-   Stay informed about any changes to counter availability.

### 3. SMS Notifications
-   Receive an SMS when your turn is approaching.
-   Notification when you are called to a counter.
-   SMS confirmation when joining and leaving the queue.

### 4. Queue Departure
-   Leave the queue at any time if your plans change.
-   Automatically notify the business to keep the queue flowing smoothly.

## Advanced Capabilities

### 1. AI-Powered Assistant
-   Interact with a smart assistant for quick answers about services and queue times.
-   Intelligent routing of customer queries.

### 2. Intelligent Monitoring
-   Built-in health checks for all system components.
-   Prometheus/Grafana integration for deep system observability.
